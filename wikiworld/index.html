WikiWorld-Real

A real image + table + text dataset from Wikidata for multimodal tabular foundation models. Every row is one entity: its Wikidata properties, its Wikipedia intro and its Commons image. Wherever the prediction target leaks into the text, title or image, it is detected and repaired or the row is dropped.

How rows are built

For each task, entities of one Wikidata class that have an English Wikipedia article are sampled with a fixed seed (up to 5,000). Columns are Wikidata properties (quantities SI-normalised), the text is the article intro, the image is the entity's Commons image (P18). Train / val / test splits come from a hash of the Wikidata ID, so an entity never appears in two splits.

How leaks are handled

  • Text: the target is matched in many surface forms (1,234,567 · 1.2 million · 1880s · ±10 years · feet for metres · "aged 94" with a death year · class labels, aliases, synonyms). The bracketed part or the whole sentence is removed and the text re-checked; no placeholder is left.
  • Title, image filename or text read inside the image (OCR) contains the target: the row is dropped.
  • A column that alone predicts the target (score ≥ 0.95) is dropped.

Sources and licenses

Structured data: Wikidata (CC0). Text: English Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0); each card links its article revision. Images: Wikimedia Commons, shown as small thumbnails; each card credits the photo's author and license and links its file page. Some images come via Wikimedia's WIT release.

Probe scores: simple models on frozen features (DINOv3 block-4 tokens and e5 layer-6 tokens as the model consumes them, plus SigLIP 2), trained on train, scored on val + test. R² for numbers, balanced accuracy for classes.
